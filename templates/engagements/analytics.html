Engagement Quality Index

For each active engagement, the share of its sign-off-bearing sections (Client Acceptance, Risk Assessment, Entity Understanding, Analytical Review, Trial Balance, Financial Statements, Directors' Statement, Audit Opinion, Income Tax, Deferred Tax, Finalisation Checklist, and every Substantive Procedures area - whichever of these already exist for it) that are at least reviewed (a Partner's own preparation counts) and, separately, partner-signed. A practical, always-current progress signal built from the sign-offs already captured everywhere else in the app - not a formal quality control conclusion, and not a substitute for the firm's own EQCR/ISQM judgement on any one engagement. Sorted lowest-reviewed first, so what most needs attention surfaces at the top.
