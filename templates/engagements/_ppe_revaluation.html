6. Revaluation to fair value (independent valuation) {% if rv.applied %}Applied{% if rv.journal_ref %} - {{ rv.journal_ref }} (REVAL){% endif %} {% elif rec and rec.lines %}Draft - nothing posted{% endif %}

Enter the valuer's fair value for each asset (or a total for a class, below). The app works out the surplus or deficit against the carrying amount at the year end, eliminates the accumulated depreciation against cost (IAS 16.35(b)), takes the surplus to other comprehensive income and the revaluation reserve net of deferred tax (any part that reverses an earlier loss goes to profit or loss), and posts one journal, REVAL. Applying it also moves the Asset Register, the Property, plant and equipment note, the statement of changes in equity and the cash flow statement. Undo removes it again.
