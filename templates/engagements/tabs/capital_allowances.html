Capital Allowances

The client's assets, worked asset by asset with the same capital allowance engine as Firm Finance > Tax: special initial allowance or wear and tear (straight line or reducing balance) by asset class, no allowance in the year of sale, recoupment when an asset is sold above its tax value and a scrapping allowance when it is sold below. Allowances are worked for the year ending {{ ca_target_end.strftime('%d %b %Y') if ca_target_end else '(set the period end first)' }}. Nothing here is hard-coded law: every rule below is editable and starts as "not verified" until a Partner or Admin has checked it against the Income Tax Act and ticked it.
