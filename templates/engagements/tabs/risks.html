Forensic risk assessment approach

This questionnaire is built around the Fraud Triangle - Incentive/Pressure, Opportunity and Rationalisation - rather than ordinary audit risk of material misstatement, plus the high-risk schemes most often seen in revenue & journal entries, asset misappropriation, procurement & vendor management, and payroll & expenses. Recommended process: gather intelligence (hotline tips, past findings, industry trends) → brainstorm how someone here could steal without getting caught → rate each risk below → map it against existing controls (including how easily management could override them) → target testing (e.g. Benford's Law, fuzzy matching) at the highest-rated areas. Do not apply your normal materiality threshold when rating significance - a small number can still hide a systemic fraud.
