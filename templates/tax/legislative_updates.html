File an instrument, or log an update

Attach the actual Act, Government Notice, Statutory Instrument, court Case or Publication (PDF, JPG or PNG) to get an automatic AI summary below - or leave the file blank and just log the update as text, as before. A Case can be Zimbabwean or foreign - the AI assesses a foreign judgment (e.g. South African) for persuasive authority rather than dismissing it for not being Zimbabwean law; you confirm the final authority classification. A Publication is a journal article or technical note (e.g. a SAIT Tax Chronicles Monthly issue) rather than Zimbabwean legislation or a judgment - it's summarised as commentary/analysis on its own terms, not forced into a legislative framing; use Source / reference for its citation (e.g. "Tax Chronicles Monthly, Issue 90, January 2026, SAIT").
