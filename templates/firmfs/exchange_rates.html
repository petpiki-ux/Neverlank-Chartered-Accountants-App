The RBZ interbank figures are the bid, ask and average. The average (mid) is used unless you choose otherwise; the bid and ask are always kept. Tax computations (QPD) use the ZIMRA rate first and fall back to the RBZ; a rate you type wins over both for the statements. If the website is down or its layout changes, the fetch stops with a message and you can still type rates in below.
