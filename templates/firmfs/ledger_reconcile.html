If a line differs

The ledger is built from the same records as the statements, plus your posted journals, so a difference points to a real cause:

  • Receivables: an invoice paid more than its total shows a credit balance in the ledger; the statements show nil for it. Check the receipts on that invoice.
  • ZWG items: the ledger translates each item at the rate of the year-end that covers its date; the statements translate every balance at this year-end's rate. A difference is an exchange difference that is not yet journalised.
  • Receipts and payments dated differently from the invoice or bill they settle only matter when the dates fall in different years and rates.
  • Receipts for an invoice that is not Sent or Paid (draft or cancelled) are in the cash book but not in the receivables.
  • Expense categories or services remapped to another statement line move the ledger figure but not the statement's derived figure.

When the financial statements balance, the clearing accounts net to nil between them: what one holds against the real accounts, another holds the other way. They show what still needs a journal, for example a loan received that is in the cash book but whose liability was typed on the year-end page. To clear one, post a journal from the clearing account to the real account and remove the figure typed on the year-end page, so it is not counted twice.
