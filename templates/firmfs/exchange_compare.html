The RBZ publishes a bid, an ask and a mid (average) each working day. A Tax rate is "the same" when it equals the mid to four decimals (or the mid rounded to fewer). Weekends and public holidays have no RBZ file, so those dates show "No RBZ reading". Nothing is copied between the two: the engine reads both. Statements use a rate you typed, else the RBZ's, else the Tax rate (switch the order on the Exchange rates page); Tax (QPD) uses the ZIMRA rate first.
