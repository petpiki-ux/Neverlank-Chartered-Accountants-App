Deferred tax is the tax rate ({{ '%g'|format(ye.tax_rate_percent or 0) }}%) on the temporary differences between the carrying amount of an item in the accounts and its tax base. For property, plant and equipment the carrying amount is the Asset register's net book value and the tax base is the tax value from Capital allowances. A carrying amount above the tax base gives a deferred tax liability; below it, an asset.
