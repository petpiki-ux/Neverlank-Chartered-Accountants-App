How the statements are put together

From the app, on an accrual basis: revenue is the invoices issued (Sent or Paid) excluding VAT, less credit notes; expenses come from the Expense register by bill date whether paid or not, filed under their categories; fees receivable are what clients still owe at the year-end; payables are the unpaid expense bills; money received from clients before invoicing is a liability (customer advances), never revenue; depreciation, asset values and the profit or loss on disposals come from the Asset Register ({{ asset_count }} asset(s) in it).

Entered for each year-end: bank and cash, other assets and liabilities, loans, VAT payable, income tax expense and payable, share capital, dividends and the opening retained earnings. Everything is in USD; a ZWG item is translated at the rate entered on the year-end and the rate is stated in the notes. Each statement carries its own checks: the balance sheet must balance, the cash flow must agree to cash, and opening retained earnings must equal the previous closing.
