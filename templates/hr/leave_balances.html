Everyone's balances

Annual/Vacation Leave only - the one leave type the firm tracks a per-employee balance for (Sick/ Special/Maternity are provisions, see above). Set a one-time opening balance per employee - "Brought forward" for an opening balance (e.g. migrating from a previous system) and "Already taken" for leave the employee took before the firm started using this Leave feature, so their available balance is correct from day one instead of overstating what they actually have left. Automatic accrual and Time Sheet leave capture only ever ADD on top of whatever is set here - they never reduce or reset it.
