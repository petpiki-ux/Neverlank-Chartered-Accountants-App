Standard Chart of Accounts (Neverlank Standard)

The firm's own numbered account list - nothing here is externally mandated, it's a drafted starting point for the firm to adjust. Picking one of these accounts when mapping a client's trial balance (in an engagement's Trial Balance tab, or in a client's QPD estimator) fills in both a firm-standard account number and the correct IAS 1 category in one step. This is separate from an Accounting engagement's own General Ledger Chart of Accounts, which is built up fresh for each bookkeeping engagement.
