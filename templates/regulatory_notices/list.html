Regulatory Notices

RBZ and FIU Zimbabwe adverse-notice PDFs, uploaded by the firm and read automatically during Sanctions & Adverse Notice Screening.

UN, OFAC and EU each publish a free, official sanctions list the app can check automatically. Neither the Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe nor Zimbabwe's Financial Intelligence Unit does — RBZ only posts occasional PDF "Public Notices" and FIU Zimbabwe has no searchable list at all — so instead, upload the notices the firm receives or finds here once. The app extracts the text from each PDF automatically, and every "Auto-screen" run on an engagement's Client Acceptance tab searches that text for a name match, in place of a live feed. A scanned/image-only PDF is still kept on file, but has no selectable text to search — add a note listing the key names it covers instead.
