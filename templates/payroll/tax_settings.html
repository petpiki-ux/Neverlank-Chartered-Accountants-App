How PAYE is calculated

PAYE uses the standard marginal (progressive) rate method: each band below taxes only the slice of taxable income that falls within it, not the whole income at that band's rate. The bands are applied edge-to-edge (each band's upper bound is the next band's lower bound), and a band with no upper bound taxes everything above its lower bound at its own rate.

Worked example (Monthly, ZIMRA 2024 USD table) - a taxable income of $1,800:

SliceAmountRateTax
$0 - $100$100.000%$0.00
$100 - $300$200.0020%$40.00
$300 - $1,000$700.0025%$175.00
$1,000 - $1,800$800.0030%$240.00
Total PAYE$455.00

This matches ZIMRA's own published worked example for a $1,800 Monthly taxable income exactly. AIDS Levy is then charged as a % of this PAYE figure, and NSSA is calculated separately on gross pay (both below) - see payroll_calc.py for the exact arithmetic used on every payslip.

PAYE Bands, by pay frequency

ZIMRA publishes a genuinely different band table for each pay frequency (Fortnightly/Weekly are NOT simply the Monthly table divided by 2 or 4), so each frequency below keeps its own independent set of bands - a payslip always uses only the table matching that employee's own pay frequency. Enter each table edge-to-edge (band N's upper bound = band N+1's lower bound) exactly as pre-loaded below, and leave "Upper bound" blank for the open-ended top band.
