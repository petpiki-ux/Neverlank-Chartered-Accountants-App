What counts as Insurable Earnings (for NSSA)

Per the NSSA Act [Chapter 17:04] and Statutory Instrument 393 of 1993, NSSA's employee and employer contributions are calculated on "Insurable Earnings", not just basic salary:

  1. Basic Salary/Wages - the core contractual wage.
  2. Regular Allowances & Cash Earnings - any cash allowance regularly earned as part of employment (e.g. housing, transport, performance bonuses) - every Allowance line item on a payslip, whether or not it's taxable for PAYE purposes (that's a separate question). A tax-exempt bonus entered as an Exempt Income item still counts here too, since it's still a cash earning.
  3. Cash Value of Benefits - the taxable cash value of a benefit in kind or employer-provided perk, entered as an Allowance item.

Excluded: an employer's own share of a benefit such as medical aid is a non-cash fringe benefit to the employer, not part of the employee's earnings, so it's never added. If instead the employee's own share of such a benefit is deducted from their pay (a Deduction line item), Insurable Earnings are still based on gross pay before that deduction - Deduction items never reduce them.

In this app, Insurable Earnings = gross pay = basic salary + all Allowance items + all Exempt Income items, capped at the Insurable Earnings ceiling above - see payroll_calc.py for the exact calculation.

How PAYE is calculated

Following ZIMRA's own published method:

  1. Gross income = basic salary + taxable and non-taxable allowances + any Exempt Income line items (e.g. a bonus exemption) entered on the payslip.
  2. Less exempt income - Exempt Income items are carved back out here, so they're never taxed, even though they're part of gross/net pay.
  3. Less allowable deductions - the employee's own NSSA contribution is deducted here too, before tax is calculated (not just taken from net pay afterwards) - gives Taxable Income.
  4. Taxable Income is run through the marginal (progressive) rate bands below - each band taxes only the slice of taxable income that falls within it, not the whole income at that band's rate - giving PAYE before credits.
  5. Less tax credits - any Tax Credit line items on the payslip (e.g. an elderly/blind/ disabled person's or medical expenses credit) are subtracted here, floored at zero.
  6. AIDS Levy (3% by default, editable above) is charged on the resulting PAYE (after credits).

Exempt Income and Tax Credit are added as their own line items on each payslip (Payslip > "Allowances, Deductions, Exempt Income & Tax Credits") - their exact CURRENT statutory amounts couldn't be reliably confirmed when this module was built, so nothing is pre-filled; enter them per employee once confirmed against ZIMRA.
