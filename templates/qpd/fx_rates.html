FX Rates (USD:ZWG)

Firm-wide dated USD:ZWG (ZiG) exchange rates - e.g. the RBZ auction or interbank rate - logged here so QPD instalments can convert the ZWG-denominated portion of the dual-currency split (a USD-equivalent figure) into an actual ZWG currency amount payable. Each instalment freezes whichever rate was most recently logged on or before the date it was (re)computed, so a later rate never retroactively changes an instalment already computed or paid.
