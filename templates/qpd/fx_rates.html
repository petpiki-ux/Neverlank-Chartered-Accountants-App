FX Rates (USD:ZWG)

Firm-wide dated USD:ZWG (ZiG) exchange rates - e.g. the RBZ auction or interbank rate - logged here so QPD instalments can convert the ZWG-denominated portion of the dual-currency split (a USD-equivalent figure) into an actual ZWG currency amount payable. Each instalment freezes whichever rate was most recently logged on or before the date it was (re)computed, so a later rate never retroactively changes an instalment already computed or paid.

Convert ZWL rates to ZWG

For rates captured while the currency was still ZWL (quoted as ZWL per 1 USD): each selected rate is divided by the conversion factor (ZWG 1 = ZWL {{ '{:,.4f}'.format(zwl_factor_default) }}) to give ZWG per 1 USD. Nothing changes until you review the preview and confirm. Each converted rate remembers its original ZWL figure, can't be converted twice, and can be undone with its "undo" link.

Leave both dates blank to convert average rates only.

The file needs a header row with "Date" and "Rate" columns (an optional "Source" and "Notes" column are picked up too); any other columns are ignored.
