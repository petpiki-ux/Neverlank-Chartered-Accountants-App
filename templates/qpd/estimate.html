Currency Split (USD / ZWG)

ZIMRA's rule on splitting provisional tax between currencies is not a plain read of the actual mix both ways: if more than 50% of revenue was earned in USD, the tax is split evenly 50:50 USD/ZWG (ZiG) regardless of exactly how far over 50% USD was; if ZWG is instead the majority currency, the split follows the ACTUAL proportion earned (e.g. 30% USD/70% ZWG stays 30:70). Set the actual % of revenue earned in USD above to see the split below - it applies to Revenue, (implied) Expenses, Taxable Income, Tax Charge and each instalment.
