How these numbers are worked out
Which invoices count
Invoices marked Sent or Paid. Drafts and Cancelled invoices are ignored. Each currency is analysed on its own.
Revenue
Invoice value excluding VAT, by invoice date. Expenses should likewise be entered excluding any VAT the firm can claim back, so profit compares like with like.
Cash collected
Invoice value excluding VAT, in the month the invoice was marked Paid (the "date paid" you chose). Mark invoices Paid with the right date for this to be accurate.
Debtors days (DSO)
What clients owe (incl. VAT) at a date, divided by the last {{ payload.dso_window }} days of invoicing (incl. VAT), multiplied by {{ payload.dso_window }}. It is roughly how many days of sales are sitting unpaid.
Ageing
Unpaid invoices grouped by how many days they are past their due date.
Creditor days
The same calculation on the firm's own unpaid bills.
Comparison arrows
Compare with the period of the same length immediately before the one selected.
