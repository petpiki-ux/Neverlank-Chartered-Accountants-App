Section 16(1) rules

How each expense type is treated. Clause letters and treatments are held here as data because the Act is amended often and published summaries disagree on lettering.
A Partner or Admin must check each rule against the current Income Tax Act [Chapter 23:06] and mark it verified. Changing a clause, treatment or the on/off switch removes the verification. Until a rule is verified, every schedule line that relies on it is flagged. The cap on passenger motor vehicles and the thin-capitalisation ratio are not here: they are entered on each engagement.
Starting values follow ZIMRA's published summary of section 16 and the common description of the thin capitalisation rule; they are a starting point, not legal advice.
