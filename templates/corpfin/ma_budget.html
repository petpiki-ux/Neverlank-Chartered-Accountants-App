The budget is the approved plan for the year. The forecast is the latest view (usually the budget revised for what has happened). Both can come from a forecast model, be typed as annual figures phased over the months, or be adjusted month by month. Model snapshots also bring a balance sheet, so the balance sheet and cash flow can be compared too.
