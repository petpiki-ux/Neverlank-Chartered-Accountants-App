Loan facilities

The bank facilities the business carries: lender, amount, interest rate, establishment fee, term and how it is repaid. Each one is scheduled month by month below, and when this pack is linked to a forecast model the model carries exactly these loans, so the forecast's interest, establishment fees, capital repayments, cash and borrowings follow them. Interest is charged each month on the balance outstanding (rate ÷ 12); in the month of drawdown it is charged only for the days from the drawdown date to the month end (the drawdown day counts). The establishment fee is expensed in the month the facility is drawn (amount × fee % plus any fixed fee). A facility drawn before the forecast starts opens at the balance outstanding then and its fee, already paid, is not charged again. Amounts are in {{ cur }}.
