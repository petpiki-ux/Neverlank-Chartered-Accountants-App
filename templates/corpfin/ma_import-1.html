Import a corrected Excel pack

Download the pack with Excel pack, correct or update it in Excel, then upload it here and the app will hold that version. Make your changes on the Data sheet (the blue cells): the statements are formulas on top of it. You can correct any month's income statement or balance sheet, the budget, the forecast, the prior year and the commentary, and change the reporting month on the Pack sheet. You will see exactly what differs from the pack, and nothing changes until you confirm.

Or upload a one-page export. The Income statement, Balance sheet, Actuals and Budget & forecast pages (“Excel: this page”) can be corrected and uploaded on their own: the Actual column is read (on the Income statement and Balance sheet pages), or the month-by-month table (on Actuals and Budget & forecast), and everything else in the pack is left as it is. Totals and variances are calculated, so correct the individual lines.
