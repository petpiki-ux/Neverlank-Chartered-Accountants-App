Model months from the prior year

For months with no books yet (for example to test a funding request): each month = last year's figure x (1 + growth) / 12, so 9 months is 9/12 of last year grown. Balances move to year end x (1 + growth); cash or overdraft balances the sheet, so a funding gap shows as an overdraft.
