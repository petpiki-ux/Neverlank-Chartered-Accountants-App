6. Loans

Existing borrowings (opening balance) and new facilities (drawdown). Enter the start date (drawdown) and end date (last repayment); the model works in months. Interest is charged on the balance outstanding, and the establishment fee is paid when the loan is drawn, on top of interest, and charged to finance costs. "Equal capital" repays evenly by month from the start to the end date (after any grace period); "bullet" repays everything at the end date. After year 1 the model runs annually, so a part-year loan carries interest only for the months it is outstanding.
