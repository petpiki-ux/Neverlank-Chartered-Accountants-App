What this module does
Forecast model
Enter the drivers (growth, margins, working-capital days, capex, loans, dividends). The income statement, balance sheet and cash flow follow - monthly in year 1, then annually, for 1 to 10 years. The balance sheet balances by construction and is checked on every run.
Scenarios
Each scenario is a set of adjustments to the base case (for example revenue -10%, receivable days +15). Compare them side by side or run a two-way sensitivity grid.
Loans & covenants
Loan balances and interest feed the statements. Set covenant limits (debt service cover, interest cover, gearing, current ratio, net debt / EBITDA) and see every year pass or breach.
Valuation
Build a discount rate (CAPM / WACC), value the free cash flows (DCF), cross-check with trading multiples and see the ranges on a football field.
Management accounts
A monthly pack of income statement, balance sheet and cash flow set against budget, forecast and last year, with KPIs, variances and commentary. Actuals come from a trial balance, manual capture, or are modelled from the prior year's accounts; existing financial statements can be loaded to supply the comparatives.
Excel
Every model downloads as a workbook with live formulas - change an input in Excel and the statements, ratios and DCF recalculate.
