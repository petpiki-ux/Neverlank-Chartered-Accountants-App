Add a source

Google News search: type the words as you would in Google News. Use OR between alternatives, quotes for exact phrases, and site:zimra.co.zw to watch one website that has no feed of its own. Website feed: the address of the site's RSS or Atom feed (often ending /feed or /rss). X (Twitter) account: the username, such as @name (or paste the account's x.com address). The app reads its recent original posts. Choose accounts you know are the real ones: the app does not guess handles. X (Twitter) search: search words as you would type them on X, e.g. (IFRS OR IAASB) (standard OR exposure). Retweets are left out and results are English only. Google Alerts: create an alert at google.com/alerts, choose "Deliver to: RSS feed", and paste the feed address.

How and when it runs

  • Opening News Watch checks the sources in the background if the last check is more than a few hours old. "Look for news now" checks on demand.
  • For a regular check without anyone opening the page, add a Render Cron Job (or any timer) that calls /news-watch/cron?token=... on this site. {% if token_set %}The token is set on this server.{% else %}It needs an environment variable called NEWS_WATCH_TOKEN set on Render to a long secret of your choosing; until then that address does not respond.{% endif %}
  • {% if configured %}The AI is switched on: each new story is read, rated and summarised from its headline and snippet.{% else %}The AI is not switched on (no ANTHROPIC_API_KEY), so stories are listed by keyword without summaries.{% endif %} Set NEWS_WATCH_MODEL on Render to use a smaller, cheaper model for this job if you want to keep costs down.
  • X (Twitter): X no longer offers a free way to read posts; its API is paid per post read. {% if x_set %}The X key is set on this server.{% else %}To use X sources, create an X developer account, buy a little credit, and set an environment variable called X_BEARER_TOKEN on Render to its Bearer Token. Until then X sources are saved but report that no key is set.{% endif %} Each X source is read at most once every {{ x_gap }} hours (even when you press "Look for news now"), takes about {{ x_posts }} posts per read, and every X post is marked unconfirmed. A handful of sources costs very little a month, but watch the X credit balance.
  • International and South African stories are only kept when they could change what a Zimbabwean practice does or advises; share-price moves, stock tips and market chatter are filtered out.
  • Google's news search feed is unofficial and may change or limit heavy use; a source that fails shows its error above and the others carry on.
  • Only the headline, a short snippet and the AI's summary are kept, never the article itself. Everything links to the publisher.
