Add a task

This always adds a general to-do, not a formal engagement Task (for those, use the Tasks tab on the engagement itself, which has Preparer/Reviewer/Partner sign-off). Picking an engagement here just tags the to-do for context and keeps it confidential to people with access to that engagement; leave it on "General / not engagement-specific" for training, admin or other firm matters. Assigning a task to someone else sends them a notification (Messages inbox).

A general to-do (whether or not it references an engagement) is simple - just marked Done, with no Reviewer/Partner sign-off step. For work that needs to go through the full review chain, add it from that engagement's own Tasks tab instead.
