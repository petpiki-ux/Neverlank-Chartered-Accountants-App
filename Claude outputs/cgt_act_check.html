The text of the Act is the consolidation updated to 1 December 2024 that was supplied to the firm. The Act contains no rate of tax: section 7 sends the calculation and the rate to the Finance Act [Chapter 23:04], which was not supplied, so no rate is stated here or built into the app. The app has no CGT register; what it holds for CGT is the Tax / CGT Advisory checklist, the TX-02 and TX-03 templates and the CGT tax head in the Tax module. Nothing here is marked verified: a Partner should confirm each line against the Gazette and the Finance Acts before relying on it.
